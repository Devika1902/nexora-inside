# Project overview

## The business problem

Nexora Technologies GmbH is a fictional growing company. Employees currently receive internal announcements through separate emails and department messages. People can miss HR updates, organizational changes, company activities and events. Site Management has no standard communication calendar, central event tracker or reusable design materials.

The project creates a practical working system for an administrative communications role. It gives teams a consistent way to prepare messages, route them for review, publish information, coordinate an event and support recruitment marketing.

## The six connected parts

### 1. Corporate communication strategy

The guidelines define employee audiences, message categories, channels, cadence and approval roles. A recurring monthly calendar gives each story an owner, draft deadline, reviewer, approver and publication date. Urgent messages have an expedited route with a required retrospective record.

### 2. Employee newsletter

The December sample issue brings management announcements, department news, employee recognition, upcoming events, HR information, new starters and organizational reminders into one readable issue. A separate Word master can be duplicated for future months.

### 3. SharePoint-style intranet design

The intranet plan defines navigation for Home, News, People, Events, Resources, Teams and the Newsletter archive. It assigns page owners and review dates. Four editable PowerPoint mockups show how employees could find news, policies and contacts, event details and current calls to action. This is a design package, not a live SharePoint site or website.

### 4. Internal employee event

The fictional Annual Employee Celebration demonstrates event planning from proposal to post-event review. The pack includes a venue comparison, indicative budget, timeline, run of show, invitation and illustrative feedback report. The Excel file tracks tasks, suppliers, attendees and budget calculations.

### 5. Recruitment marketing and trade fair

The careers fair kit provides a company introduction, recruitment poster copy, candidate leaflet, stand operating concept and employee presentation outline. It gives fair staff a consistent story and an accessible, privacy-aware visitor journey. The QR destination is a reserved fictional URL and must be replaced before real use.

### 6. Communication performance and administration

The Excel workbook has a dashboard, formula-driven event budget, and filterable announcement, event, task, people, supplier and registration records. The records contain more than 500 rows apiece where specified, making them useful for sorting, filtering, pivot-style reporting practice and portfolio demonstrations.

## How the parts work together

```text
Communication guidelines
          ↓
Editorial calendar → Newsletter and intranet pages → Employee action
          ↓                                      ↓
       Event plan → Invitation and registration → Attendance and feedback
          ↓
      Tracker and review → Improvements for the next communication cycle
          ↓
Recruitment story → Careers fair materials → Candidate next steps
```

## Suggested walkthrough

1. Explain the communication problem and show how the guidelines assign owners and approval steps.
2. Open the newsletter issue and identify how each required content category appears in one repeatable format.
3. Use the PowerPoint to walk through the proposed intranet pages and explain page ownership.
4. Present the event proposal, show the budget formulas and filter the registration and task sheets.
5. Show the recruitment materials and describe the trade fair visitor journey.
6. Finish with the dashboard and explain what the organization could learn from timing, approvals, attendance, expenditure and feedback.

## Success measures to use in a real organization

- Share of planned communications published on time.
- Time from complete draft to approval.
- Employee reach by audience and channel.
- Completion of calls to action such as registrations or required acknowledgements.
- Current-page ownership and review coverage.
- Event attendance against registrations and venue capacity.
- Final event spend against the approved budget.
- Event feedback and actions assigned after the event.

The sample workbook contains invented values for practicing analysis. It does not claim real organizational results.
